FineRS++ builds a Qwen2.5-VL-based global-local perception framework with coarse-to-fine MLLM reinforcement learning, GSE + LPR training, and a GRPO localization-aware backtracking reward. On FINERS-4k, it achieves gIoU **55.1**, cIoU **46.5**, MVQA **83.3%**, and OVQA **56.7%**.
