DreamMix addresses the tension between identity preservation and text-driven attribute editing in subject-driven image inpainting. It introduces an Attribute Decoupling Mechanism, Textual Attribute Substitution, and a disentangled local-global inpainting framework.

The method reaches a CLIP-T score of **0.289** on SDXL and **0.290** on FLUX, with only 4–6 reference images needed for adaptation. The project has received **100+ GitHub stars**.
