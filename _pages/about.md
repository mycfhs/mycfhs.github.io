# About Me

Hello! I’m **YiCheng Yang (杨镒丞)**, a master’s student in **Information and Communication Engineering** at **Dalian University of Technology**. My work sits at the intersection of **end-to-end autonomous driving, world models, computer vision, diffusion models, and multimodal large language models**.

I enjoy taking ideas from research prototypes to measurable, deployable systems. During my internships, I worked on world-model-based driving, end-to-end parking scoring models, temporal modeling, real-vehicle deployment, and automated evaluation. My research interests include controllable image generation, high-resolution visual reasoning and segmentation, and robust perception in challenging environments.
